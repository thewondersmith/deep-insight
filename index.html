The Shift

Same analyst. Far less drowning.

You already have the judgment. What you don’t have is the hours. Deep Insight shows you how to hand the grunt work to AI, the reading, the sorting, the formatting, so you spend your time on the analysis only a human can do. These aren’t hypotheticals. They’re workflows from the book.

1 week → 1 hour
Risk review · corporate intel

An analyst’s NLP workflow turned a full week of reading filings and news into about an hour, and flagged risks the manual pass would have missed.

+6 hrs / week
Reporting · maritime workflow

Automating the report formatting won back six hours every week and got finished intelligence out the door two days early.

AI is not here to replace you. It’s here to free you up to perform deeper analysis.
The thesis of Deep Insight
  • Map your own workflow and pinpoint exactly where AI saves you the most time.
  • Automate collection and triage so you analyze instead of gather.
  • Use NLP to pull entities, sentiment, and themes from oceans of text in minutes.
  • Write prompts that return usable intelligence, not vague, generic summaries.
  • Run predictive analysis to flag threats before they fully surface.
  • Interrogate bias and the black box so you can defend a finding in a briefing, or a courtroom.
  • Validate AI output and trust nothing until it’s proven.
  • Read the horizon, from agentic to multimodal AI, before adversaries exploit it.

About The Author

Written from the field

Rae Baker is a Manager of Cyber Defense and Resilience at Deloitte and a senior open source intelligence analyst, a licensed private investigator, and a maritime OSINT expert. She has spoken at DEF CON, ShmooCon, and the SANS OSINT Summit.

She is the author of Wiley’s Deep Dive. Deep Insight turns that same practical, real world approach loose on AI, so the next generation of analysts can work faster without giving up the judgment that matters.
