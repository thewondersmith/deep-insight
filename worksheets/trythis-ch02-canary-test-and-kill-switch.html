Canary Test and Kill Switch Builder

Know when your AI starts drifting, and know exactly what to do about it.

Tools don't fail during business hours. They fail at 4am on a holiday weekend, mid-crisis. Any AI you bring into a workflow needs two guardrails: a canary test that tells you when it starts to drift, and a kill switch that says exactly what happens when it does.
